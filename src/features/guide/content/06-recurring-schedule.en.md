## Leaving out the same times every time?

When you join many events, the same blocks keep getting left out—"class every Monday and Wednesday evening," "always busy on weekend mornings." **My Schedule** lets you register these recurring commitments just once. After you set it up, your availability is filled in automatically when you join an event, so you don't have to paint the grid from scratch every time.

My Schedule has two parts: your daily **sleep time**, and a **weekly fixed schedule** marking the slots you're always busy on each weekday. Together they define when you're free.

![The My Schedule screen on My Page — sleep hours and weekly recurring commitments at a glance](/images/guide/my-schedule/overview-en.png 'My Schedule screen')

> Caution: My Schedule is available **only to logged-in users**. Your schedule is saved to your account, so it doesn't apply when you join as a guest. If you coordinate meetings often, we recommend logging in.

## Open the edit screen

From My Page, open **My Schedule** and tap **Edit** to open the editor. Here you set your sleep time and weekly fixed schedule in turn.

![The My Schedule editor — sleep time and the weekly board shown in order](/images/guide/my-schedule/edit-en.png 'My Schedule editor')

## Set your sleep time

First, set the hours you sleep each day. Pick a **start time** and an **end time** from the dropdowns. For example, set 23:00 – 07:00 and that range is automatically left out of your availability in every event. If you set the start and end to the same time, it's treated as no sleep time.

![Setting sleep time with start and end dropdowns](/images/guide/my-schedule/sleep-en.png#sm 'Setting sleep time')

## Mark your weekly fixed schedule

The board below is a grid with time slots across and **weekdays (Mon–Sun)** down. Tap the cells you're always busy each week. Paint in recurring commitments like classes, work shifts, and regular meetings. Tap a cell again to clear it if you tapped it by mistake.

![Painting recurring busy slots on the weekday board](/images/guide/my-schedule/weekly-en.png 'Weekly fixed schedule board')

## Save

When you're done, tap **Save** at the top to finish. Your saved My Schedule is kept on your account and applied automatically to any event you join afterward. Whenever your routine changes, come back to this screen to update it.
