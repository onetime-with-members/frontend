## No need to paint each class by hand

Painting the same class hours onto the board one by one is tedious. If you're a student, you can import the timetable you've already built in **Everytime**. Just paste the link, and your class hours are filled into your weekly fixed schedule automatically—one import per semester and your My Schedule is done.

![My timetable as set up in the Everytime app](/images/guide/everytime-import/source-en.png#sm 'My Everytime timetable')

![An Everytime timetable reflected directly in the weekly fixed schedule](/images/guide/everytime-import/result-en.png 'Reflected in the weekly board')

> Tip: Already have a My Schedule set up? No problem. Importing your Everytime timetable **keeps your existing weekly fixed schedule intact** and simply paints your class hours on top of it. Nothing you've already marked is erased, so import with confidence.

## Open the import screen

You import your Everytime timetable from the **My Schedule edit screen**. Open it from **My Schedule** on My Page, or jump straight to it from the **My Schedule** section on the home screen once you're logged in.

![The Add Everytime timetable button in the My Schedule section on the home screen](/images/guide/everytime-import/home-entry-en.png#lg 'Access from the home My Schedule section')

![The Add Everytime timetable button at the top of the My Schedule editor](/images/guide/everytime-import/entry-en.png#lg 'Access from the My Schedule edit page')

Tapping the button opens the **Everytime import screen**, where you paste the link.

![The Everytime import screen that opens after tapping the button](/images/guide/everytime-import/screen-en.png#lg 'The opened Everytime import screen')

## Paste the timetable link

Open your timetable in the Everytime app, copy the link with **'Share URL'**, and paste it into the input field. Tap confirm, and the server reads your timetable and fills your class hours into the weekly schedule.

![The Everytime timetable link pasted into the input field](/images/guide/everytime-import/paste-en.png#lg 'Pasting the Everytime link')

> Caution: To import from Everytime, briefly change your timetable's visibility to **'Public'** (settings icon → change visibility → Public). While it's private, pasting the link won't let it read your timetable. Once imported, you can switch it back to private.

## Check and save

Make sure the imported timetable landed correctly on the weekly board. For commitments Everytime doesn't have—like a private academy or a part-time job—paint them on the board by hand. When you're done adjusting, tap **Save** at the top to finish.
