## Open the event link

Participants start by opening the **event link** they received from the organizer. The link shows the event's candidate dates (or weekdays) and time range as a time grid. At first—before anyone has added their availability—the grid is empty, and participants fill it in with their available times one by one.

![The time grid shown after opening an event link — candidate dates and time slots laid out in a grid](/images/guide/adding-availability/grid-en.png 'Event time grid')

## Join by logging in or as a guest

Before marking your times, you can choose one of two ways to join.

- **Log in** — you skip the name-and-password screen below and go straight to schedule entry.
- **Join as a guest** — enter your name on the screen below and jump in without logging in. Handy for a quick, one-time response.

![The name and password entry screen guests see — logged-in users skip it and go straight to schedule entry](/images/guide/adding-availability/login-en.png#sm 'Guest name & password entry (skipped when logged in)')

## Mark your available times on the grid

Now mark the **times you are available** on the grid.

1. Tap a cell, or press and **drag** to select several cells at once.
2. Scroll up, down, left, and right to see every date and time slot.
3. Tap a cell again to deselect it if you picked it by mistake.

![Several cells selected by dragging across the time grid to mark availability — selected cells highlighted](/images/guide/adding-availability/marking-en.png#sm 'Dragging to mark availability')

To make overlaps easier to find, mark your availability generously and only leave out the times you truly can't make. If everyone marks the bare minimum, there are fewer overlapping slots to choose from.

## Save

When you're done marking, save. Your times are added to the tally immediately, so the organizer and other participants can see them on the results screen. If you logged in, you can come back and edit your schedule any time.

![The results screen right after saving — your available times counted in the tally with recommended times shown, and an Edit Schedule button to change them](/images/guide/adding-availability/save-en.png 'Your saved times reflected in the results')
