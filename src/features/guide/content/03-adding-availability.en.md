## Open the event link

Participants start by opening the **event link** they received from the organizer. The link shows the event's candidate dates (or weekdays) and time range as a time grid.

![The empty time grid shown after opening an event link — candidate dates and time slots laid out in a grid](/images/guide/adding-availability-grid-en.png)

## Join by logging in or as a guest

Before marking your times, you can choose one of two ways to join.

- **Log in** — your name is auto-filled and your schedule is saved to your account, making it easy to edit later.
- **Join as a guest** — just enter your name and jump in without logging in. Handy for a quick, one-time response.

![The join-method screen showing a log-in button alongside a guest name input field](/images/guide/adding-availability-login-en.png)

## Mark your available times on the grid

Now mark the **times you are available** on the grid.

1. Tap a cell, or press and **drag** to select several cells at once.
2. Scroll up, down, left, and right to see every date and time slot.
3. Tap a cell again to deselect it if you picked it by mistake.

![Several cells selected by dragging across the time grid to mark availability — selected cells highlighted](/images/guide/adding-availability-marking-en.png)

To make overlaps easier to find, mark your availability generously and only leave out the times you truly can't make. If everyone marks the bare minimum, there are fewer overlapping slots to choose from.

## Save

When you're done marking, save. Your times are added to the tally immediately, so the organizer and other participants can see them on the results screen. If you logged in, you can come back and edit your schedule any time.
